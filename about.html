About Royal Tech Solutions

Founded in 2022, Royal Tech Solutions (Pty) Ltd has grown into a trusted IT partner for small, medium, and corporate enterprises in South Africa. We are proudly headquartered in Germiston and operate across the region.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To empower businesses with reliable, affordable, and innovative technology solutions.

Vision: To become a trusted IT partner in South Africa by providing quality service and modern technology solutions.

Our History & Journey

What started as a small IT consultancy out of a home office in Germiston has scaled into a full-service technology provider within just three years. We recognised early on that small and medium-sized businesses were underserved by large corporate tech firms. By focusing on responsive, personal support, we have been able to grow our reputation organically.

Today, we continue to give back to the communities we serve, offering mentorship to local IT students and providing affordable tech solutions to educational institutions.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity: We offer honest advice and transparent pricing, never overcharging our clients.
  • Innovation: We stay on top of the latest technology trends to provide modern, forward-thinking solutions.
  • Reliability: We answer calls quickly, show up on time, and solve problems efficiently.
  • Client Success: Your business goals are our business goals. We only succeed when you succeed.

Our Team & Presence

We currently employ over 50 dedicated IT professionals, engineers, and support staff across our two main branches:

  • Head Office: 10 Tech Park, Germiston, Johannesburg, 1401
  • Regional Office: 25 Innovation Street, Makhado, Limpopo, 0920

Having successfully serviced over 200 clients, our team is equipped to handle everything from a single computer repair to a complete network overhaul for a corporate office.
